## Website goals and objectives
- Increase online sales
- Make it easier for customers to find products
-	Improve shopping experience
-	Attract more visitors to the website
-	Encourage customers to return and shop again
-	Build trust by providing secure online payments

## Key features and functionality
### Features
-	Home
-	About us
-	Products
-	Categories
-	Deals
-	Contact us

### Functionality
-	Navigation: Users can move between the Home, About Us, Products, Deals, Enquiry, FAQ and Contact pages using the navigation menu. 
-	Product browsing: Users can view different technology products and their descriptions, prices and images on the Products page. 
-	Deals: Users can view special offers and discounted products on the Deals page. 
-	Enquiry form: Users can enter their name, email, phone number and enquiry message and submit the form. 
-	Contact form: Users can enter their contact details and send a message to AetherTech. 
-	FAQ: Users can view answers to common questions about products, delivery, payments and the company. 
-	Contact information: Users can view AetherTech's email, phone number, business hours and locations. 
-	Maps: Users can view the two AetherTech locations through embedded maps. 
-	External navigation: Links such as "View Products", "View Deals", "Make an Enquiry" and "Contact Us" take users to the relevant pages.
